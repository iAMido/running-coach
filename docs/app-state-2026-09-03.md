## Part 1 — What the app is

### The platform

One Next.js 16 app hosting three things that share a deployment and a Supabase
project but almost nothing else:

| App | Route | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **RunCoach** | `/coach/*` | AI running coach. The subject of this document. |
| **CalTrack** | `/caltrack/*` | Calorie / weight tracking. Separate schema, separate client. |
| **CV + blog** | `/`, `/blog/*` | Public portfolio. |

**Both apps are staying** (confirmed by the athlete, 2026-09-03). One Supabase
project `tlnqkxwlrewbtufnqiwi`, two schemas: `public` for CalTrack, `runcoach`
for RunCoach. Never mix the clients — `lib/db/supabase.ts` for RunCoach,
`lib/db/supabase-caltrack.ts` for CalTrack.

### RunCoach's core job

Take the athlete's real training data, judge it honestly, and coach against a
goal — **without ever inventing a number it does not have.** That last clause is
not decoration. Nearly every serious bug this codebase has produced was a value
that looked measured and was not, and the whole design is now shaped around
telling *measured*, *absent* and *not-applicable* apart.

### Where data comes from

**intervals.icu is the only live provider** (Garmin-sourced). Three sync paths,
all through one function `syncIntervalsForUser`:

- `POST /api/intervals/sync` — the "Sync Now" button, and sync-on-open (30-min debounce)
- Vercel crons at 15:00 and 21:40 UTC daily

**Strava is disarmed, not removed.** `strava_tokens` is empty, its crons are
gone from `vercel.json`, all 117 formerly-Strava runs now carry `icu_`
filenames, and as of today **its nav link is gone** — the page and all routes
remain reachable at `/coach/strava`, and the refresh token is preserved in
`runcoach._bak_20260806_strava_tokens`.

`lib/ingest/upsert-run.ts` is the single owner of per-activity mapping for every
provider. Updates are fill-null-only and always preserve the row `id`
(`run_feedback` and `laps` both cascade on delete).

## Part 2 — What the app does now

### Three adaptation loops, three clocks

Deliberately separate mechanisms. Do not collapse them.

| Loop | Cadence | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Readiness** | daily | Today only — GO / EASY / REST. Never rewrites the plan. |
| **Micro** | Saturday 18:00 UTC | Re-shape the next 1–3 weeks inside the current block. **Proposes; never applies.** |
| **Macro** | per block / on trigger | Phase targets and remaining season shape. |

Saturday rather than Sunday because the training week runs Sunday–Saturday, so
the proposal lands after the week closes and *before* Sunday's session — the
only moment it can change anything.

### Planning is now two layers

**Season (`runcoach.macro_plans`)** holds intent, not workouts: phases with
weekly km and vert ranges, the capability being built, and **exit criteria**. A
phase advances when its criteria hold, not when its weeks elapse — so a slow
adapter extends base rather than being marched into a build phase. Criteria must
be checkable against `TrainingState`. `suggestedPhaseCount` scales by proportion
(1 phase under 8 weeks, 5 over 40), so 11-month, 6-month and 4-month requests
share one model.

Why no workouts at this layer: measured at **~623 output tokens per plan-week**,
48 weeks is ~30k against a 16k cap — but the real reason is that a prescribed
session for July 2027 is fiction, and a plan wrong in its details teaches you to
ignore it.

**Blocks (`runcoach.training_plans`)** carry the actual sessions, 8–16 weeks,
generated against a named phase and its exit criteria.

### Plans fit the race, not just its distance

When race elevation is supplied, `buildRaceDemandBlock` computes the gradient
and states the gap against the athlete's **own measured climbing**. For the
target race it renders: *61.9 m/km, 4.9× his steepest recorded run* — then
instructs that gradient is the limiter and distance largely in hand.

It returns nothing without elevation, so **road plans are completely
unaffected** — verified by generating a plain 12-week half marathon: 0/12 weeks
and 0/48 workouts carried elevation fields, 48/48 carried indoor alternatives,
and no session landed outside the four chosen training days.

### The signal layer

`lib/coach/training-state.ts` is the **single assembly point** read by all three
loops *and* the chat coach: weekly volume and vert buckets, adherence against
stated training days, aerobic efficiency, decoupling percentile, CTL/ATL form,
readiness, climbing baseline.

Its `gaps[]` array renders in prompts under *"WHAT COULD NOT BE MEASURED —
treat these as unknown, never as fine"*. A reader must be able to tell "training
is going well" from "we cannot see how training is going".

### What the athlete controls

- **Training days** — profile default, overridable per plan via a Sunday-first picker. Choosing per-plan does **not** rewrite the profile.
- **Goal and target time** — `/coach/settings`, plus a per-plan target-time field. Race pace is now *derived* from these, never assumed.
- **Race profile** — distance, elevation, terrain access, with live gradient shown as you type.
- **Every proposed change** — the Saturday loop proposes; you accept or dismiss.

## Part 3 — Everything that changed today

### Elevation, end to end (new capability)

- `runs.elevation_gain_m`, `elevation_loss_m`, generated `vert_per_km`; `laps.elevation_gain_m`
- Backfilled: **128 runs** with gain and loss, **1,168 lap rows** across 99 runs. Last 90 days is **42/42**.
- `lib/utils/elevation.ts` with bands calibrated to this athlete: Flat <5, Rolling 5–15, Hilly 15–25, Mountain ≥25 m/km
- Surfaced on the dashboard (Vert tile), run rows, the review, the coach prompt, the scorecard's climb row, and pushed to the watch
- Two new run types: `Vert / Hill`, `Trail Long Run`

**Probe findings that shaped it:** descent is free (both gain and loss are on the
summary payload, 128/130); VAM is unusable (9/130, so no vertical TRIMP); and
**lap gain does not sum to run gain** (130.4 m vs 210.9 m measured) because
provider laps are detected segments that do not tile the activity.

### Bugs fixed (in the order found)

1. **RAG vector search was dead for every AI call.** Four functions pinned `search_path` without `extensions`, where pgvector lives, so `<=>` was unresolvable. Book methodology, coach workouts and user resources all returned nothing — the coach ran on model priors while appearing to consult a library of 7 books and 1,452 embedded chunks.
2. **And the threshold was above the corpus maximum.** `match_threshold` 0.7 (books) and 0.65 (user resources) against a measured best-match range of **0.518–0.672**. Two unrelated faults, identical symptom, because an empty RAG layer is silent. Now 0.45, measured.
3. **Mon/Wed/Fri hardcoded in five places** — two of them *after* the training-days parameter, silently overriding it.
4. **`getDefaultProfile()` seeded `'Mon, Wed, Fri'`** into every new profile — the origin of the whole class.
5. **A frozen "sub-2hr HM = 5:40/km" pace table** fed to every plan adjustment, wrong by 27 s/km even for the goal it named, and meaningless for a mountain race.
6. **`'Sub-2hr Half Marathon'` goal fallback** in two more files.
7. **Superseded HR zones** (max 185) as fallbacks, after the athlete moved to 191.
8. **The adjustment path was years behind the others** — no training-day constraint while inviting day changes, no `COACH_STATIC_BLOCK`, "ALL remaining weeks" against an 8k cap, no elevation/indoor preservation, and an unnamed default model.
9. **`followed_plan` typed `boolean`, stored as text.** The only explicit adherence signal, read as `=== false`, which can never be true for `'no'`. Logging "I skipped it" never reached the coach.
10. **Two broken plan types** — "5K Speed" and "Maintenance" were not in the schema enum and 400'd on submit.
11. **The new plan fields had no UI** — elevation and indoor alternatives were emitted and never rendered.
12. **Climb bands drifted** between `CLIMB_BANDS` and the prompt prose after recalibration.
13. **`CLAUDE.md` named two tables that do not exist** — `book_embeddings` and `weekly_feedback`.

### Mistakes I made and corrected

Recorded because the pattern matters more than the instances.

- **Set the vert threshold to 12 m/km by interpolating between two probe buckets instead of measuring at 12.** It fired on 7 runs, not 1 — five of them ordinary easy runs sitting exactly on the p95. Moved to 15.
- **Claimed a 30-second lap guard was a no-op on existing data without checking.** It would have silently restated 79 laps across 50 runs. Scoped to the steep path only.
- **Claimed RAG was fixed after the search_path repair**, because the plan cited 80/20 and Norwegian. Those are named in the persona prompt and provable with an empty book layer. A plausible citation is not evidence of retrieval.
- **Wrote a regex that acquired an invisible `0x08` byte** from a shell-escaping slip. It matched nothing while rendering identically to the correct pattern in grep, cat and every editor. Only a char-code dump found it.

### Test suite

**71 tests, up from 0 in these areas.** The most valuable is
`lib/ai/prompt-contracts.test.ts`, which builds the real prompts and asserts
forbidden literals are absent — it would have caught items 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 12
above, and it found 12 itself.
